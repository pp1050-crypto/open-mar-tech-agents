# 🚀 Open MarTech & Agentic CRM Engine

An open-source collection of B2B/B2C marketing automation workflows, CRM lead-routing schemas, and agentic AI content systems designed for lean marketing teams, indie founders, and creators.

Built and maintained by **[Pratik Patil]** (MSc Global Marketing | B.Eng Electronics & Telecom).

---

## 🎯 Why This Project Exists
Small marketing teams and solo operators are frequently priced out of enterprise RevOps and AI automation stacks. This repository open-sources battle-tested workflows—originally built for internal commercial and residential marketing engines that **doubled lead-handling efficiency and delivered 3x campaign performance**—so anyone can deploy them using accessible APIs and LLMs.

## 📦 What's Inside

### 1. Automated Lead Engine & CRM Routing (`/crm-workflows`)
* **Apollo.io Drip & Webhook Schemas:** Automated outbound sequence structures with A/B-tested messaging frameworks.
* **Meta Lead Ads $\rightarrow$ Bigin by Zoho CRM:** Middleware routing blueprints (via Zapier/Webhooks) for zero-latency lead handoff to sales teams.
* **Lead Scoring & Sanitization:** Prompt templates and regex scripts for cleaning incoming B2B prospect data before CRM ingestion.

### 2. Agentic Content & Social Distribution (`/ai-content-agents`)
* **Multi-Channel Content Repurposing:** System prompts and Claude automation workflows to turn long-form technical case studies into LinkedIn carousels, newsletter copy, and short-form video scripts.
* **SEO & GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Audit Checklists:** Structured frameworks for auditing site architecture, on-page copy, and AI-search visibility.

### 3. Internal Micro-App Blueprints (`/internal-tools`)
* Architecture guides for building lightweight internal tools for social media scheduling, email campaign QA (HubSpot/Mailchimp/Stripo), and GDPR-compliant list management.
